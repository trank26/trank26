🎣 You’ve Been Phished!

This page is part of a **cybersecurity awareness exercise**. No information was collected — but this is how real phishing attacks can look.

What is phishing?

Phishing is when attackers pretend to be trusted organizations (like universities) to trick you into sharing personal information or clicking malicious links.

Red flags in THIS page

  • No official university domain (this could be hosted anywhere)
  • No login through a secure admissions portal
  • Generic outreach with no personalization
  • Form asks for info without verification of sender identity

How to protect yourself

  • Always check the URL carefully
  • Verify emails through official school websites
  • Never submit info through unfamiliar links
  • When in doubt, go directly to the source instead of clicking links
